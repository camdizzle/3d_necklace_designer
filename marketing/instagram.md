# Chain Studio on Instagram: launch kit

Everything here is written to copy and paste. Facts come from `promo-context.txt` and the site copy. Three rules apply to every post:

1. **The chains are 3D printed plastic with a metallic-tone finish.** Say "gold tone", never "gold". Never "metal", "plated", "solid" or "real".
2. **No delivery or production time.** If someone asks, answer with what you know for that order, in DMs. Do not put a number in a caption.
3. **Renders are labelled.** Any post that uses a file from `marketing/assets/instagram/` says "3D preview" or "render" in the caption. If you have a real photo of the same design, lead with the photo and put the render second.

**About hashtags.** Each caption has 10 hashtags in two lines: a core 5 and an extra 5. Instagram was widely reported to have cut the cap to 5 hashtags per post in December 2025 (see "Checked" at the end). Paste the core 5 first. Add the extra line only if the app lets you; if it warns or blocks, delete the extra line. Nothing in the caption depends on it.

**About links.** Links in captions are not clickable, so captions say "link in bio". Stories get a link sticker with the full URL given for that post.

## 1. Positioning

Speak to three groups. Each post is aimed at one of them, not all three.

| Audience | Who they are | One-line message |
|---|---|---|
| **Name-chain wearers** | People who like streetwear and bold name chains, gamers and streamers with a tag, teams and squads | "Put your name on a chain. Design it free in 3D, get it printed for $25 shipped." |
| **Gift buyers** | Someone buying for a dad, a graduate, a bridal party, a friend's birthday | "A gift with their name on it, designed by you in a few minutes. $25 with free shipping." |
| **3D printer owners** | Hobbyists with their own printer, including multi-colour machines | "Design a name pendant in the browser and download the STL or colour-separated 3MF for $2." |

What all three should take away: the designer is free and needs no account, the price is low because it is plastic, and you see exactly what you are ordering before you pay.

## 7. Weekly routine

About two hours a week in total.

**Monday (30 min).** Look at the admin dashboard, then schedule the week's four posts from the calendar. Swap in a real photo anywhere you now have one.

**Post days (10 min each).** Publish, then share the post to your story with a link sticker to `https://hypechain.c23d.shop/?utm_source=instagram`. Stay on the app for 15 minutes afterwards and reply to early comments.

**Daily (10 min).** Replies:

- Answer every comment and DM within a day. Use the person's name if it is visible.
- "How much?" → `It's $25 with free shipping to the US, Canada, UK and Australia. Designing is free. Link's in my bio.`
- "Is it real gold?" → `No, it's 3D printed plastic with a gold tone finish. That's why it's $25.`
