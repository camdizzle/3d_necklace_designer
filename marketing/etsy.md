# Etsy listing kit — printed chains

Three listing drafts to copy and paste, the fee arithmetic behind the prices, and the steps for turning an Etsy order into a print file.

Rules were checked on 2026-11-20. What was confirmed and what was not is in [Checked](#checked) at the end. Read that section before you publish: several points could only be confirmed from secondary sources.

Anything in `[OWNER: ...]` is a blank only you can fill. Do not publish a listing with one still in it.

---

## Read this first: five rules that shaped the copy

1. **No link to, or mention of, your own site anywhere on Etsy.** Etsy's Off-Platform Transactions policy and Seller Policy prohibit content that "encourages or facilitates an off-platform transaction", and this covers listing text, photos, and messages. So these drafts never mention hypechain.c23d.shop, never say "design it yourself online", and never quote the site's $25 price. Practical consequences:
   - Do not put the site URL, a QR code or a "cheaper on my site" note in a listing, a photo, a message, or a package insert.
   - Do not send buyers a designer share link in Etsy messages. Send a screenshot instead.
   - Check every render before uploading it: no URL, no QR code, no site watermark in the image.
   - Selling the same product on your own site is allowed. Using Etsy to send buyers there is not.
2. **The first photo must be a real photo of a finished chain.** Renders can only go in later slots, and must look like what the buyer will receive. A late-October 2026 update is reported to require at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background in every listing.
3. **3D printed items must be your own original design.** Since June 2025 Etsy does not allow printing files designed by someone else, even with a commercial licence. The pendants qualify (you build each one). **Check the chain link meshes**: if the Cuban link or Twisted Star link model was downloaded from another designer, that part does not qualify. `[OWNER: confirm both chain link meshes are your own design before listing]`
4. **Never imply metal.** Every title, tag and attribute below says "tone" or "plastic". Do not pick gold, silver or any metal under Materials.
5. **"Buy 3 get 1 free" becomes a set-of-4 listing.** Etsy's discount tools could not be confirmed to support "every 4th item free", so Listing 3 is a single listing for four chains priced at three times the single price. No discount code needed, and nothing refers to the site.

## Price guidance

### Fees used (US seller, Etsy Payments, buyer pays in USD)

| Fee | Rate | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 per listing, charged again each time it sells and renews | Third-party 2026 sources agree |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of item price + shipping charged + gift wrap | Etsy Fees & Payments Policy, quoted in search results |
| Payment processing (US) | 3% + $0.25 per order, on the total the buyer pays including sales tax | Etsy help centre and third-party 2026 sources |
| Offsite Ads | 15% of the order, only on orders that came from an Etsy offsite ad; capped at $100 per order. Shops under $10,000 in sales over the past 365 days can opt out. Above $10,000 the rate is 12% and you cannot opt out | Etsy Fees & Payments Policy, quoted in search results |

Not included, and you must check them:

- **If your bank account is not in the US**, processing rates differ (reported: UK 4% + £0.20; Canada and Australia 3% + 0.25 local currency for domestic orders, 4% for international) and a Regulatory Operating Fee applies to sellers in the UK and Canada among others. Reported rates conflict (UK 0.32% or 0.48%; Canada 0.5% or 1.15%) and Etsy raised them on 22 June 2026. Use the formula below with your own rates.
- **Sales tax** raises the processing fee slightly, because the 3% is charged on the total including tax. At 8% tax on a $28.50 chain this is about 7 cents.
- **Currency conversion** if your shop currency differs from your bank currency.
- **Etsy Ads** (onsite), if you turn them on.
- **Postage.** Shipping is free to the buyer, so you pay it. This is the same cost you carry on the site.

### Formula

```
Net = P x (1 - t - p - a) - f - L

P = listing price (shipping is free, so nothing else is added)
t = transaction fee rate        0.065
p = processing rate             0.03   (US)
a = Offsite Ads rate            0, 0.15 or 0.12
f = processing fixed fee        $0.25  (US)
L = listing fee                 $0.20

To net a target amount N:   P = (N + f + L) / (1 - t - p - a)
```

For a US seller with no Offsite Ads: **Net = 0.905 x P - 0.45**

## Turning an Etsy order into a print file

1. **Read the order.** Open it in Shop Manager > Orders. Note the personalisation answers, the two variations (chain style, finish tone) and any message from the buyer. If anything is missing or a line is too long, ask through Etsy messages before you start.
2. **Unlock the owner bypass.** In your browser open `https://hypechain.c23d.shop/design/#bypass=<your key>`, where the key is the `ADMIN_BYPASS_KEY` value set on the server. The bypass lasts for that browser tab's session, so repeat this step when you open a new browser session. Do not paste the key into Etsy, a message or a shared note.
3. **Build the design** in the designer at `/design/`:
   - Text lines 1 to 3, font per line, letter colour per line
   - Pendant shape (Outline for the house style) and base colour
   - Finish tone for the pendant; chain style and chain tone to match the variations
   - Turn on the dimensions overlay and check the size
4. **Read the print check.** Fix any warning about lettering that is too small or parts that are too thin. If the buyer's text will not print well, message them with an alternative before printing.
5. **Send a preview (optional, and only if the listing promises one).** Use the Screenshot button and attach the PNG to an Etsy message. Do not send the share link: that sends the buyer to your site, which is the thing Etsy prohibits.
6. **Export.** With the bypass active the export skips Stripe. Choose 3MF if you print in more than one colour (chain, base and each lettering colour arrive as separate coloured parts), or STL for a single-colour print.
7. **Keep a record.** Use Share Design and save the link in your own order notes next to the Etsy order number, so you can reprint the exact design if a chain arrives damaged. The link is for you, not the buyer.
8. **Set of 4.** Build the first chain, export, then change only the text for chains 2 to 4 and export each. Check all four names against the order before printing.
9. **Print, pack, ship.** Add tracking in Etsy and mark the order shipped before the ship-by date. Do not put a card, sticker or QR code for your site in the parcel. `[OWNER: if you want to include a business card, read the Off-Platform Transactions policy first; I could not confirm what Etsy allows in packaging.]`

### Not confirmed — check these yourself

1. **Exact wording of the Off-Platform Transactions policy**, and whether a bare website link is allowed in the shop's About section. Secondary sources say links are tolerated in About / social fields but not in listings. I wrote the listings with no link and no mention at all. I did not write About-section copy; if you add your site there, read the policy first.
2. **Renders as listing photos.** No Etsy rule naming 3D renders was found. The drafts treat them as "computer-generated mockups": later slots only, captioned as previews. The reported October 2026 requirement for one unedited real photo is from secondary sources. Check Etsy's "Listing Image Requirements" page.
3. **AI disclosure.** Etsy has a disclosure checkbox for generative AI. The renders are produced from your own 3D models, not by an image generator, so I did not treat it as applying. If any image was made or edited with generative AI, tick the box.
4. **Whether a chain built with your own design tool counts as "your original design".** It should, since you wrote the tool and build each pendant, but Etsy enforcement of the 3D printing rule is reported as inconsistent. The chain link meshes are the weak point: confirm they are yours.
5. **Title limit (140) and tag limits (13 x 20)** come from third-party 2026 guides, not Etsy's page. The listing form will enforce the real limit.
6. **Which personalisation system your shop has.** Both versions are provided. Etsy's developer docs gave no firm date for the 5-question system reaching all shops.
7. **Fees:** the $0.20 listing fee, the 3% + $0.25 US processing rate, and the per-extra-unit $0.20 fee are from third-party sources and my own background knowledge, not a page I could open. Non-US processing rates and the Regulatory Operating Fee are reported inconsistently. Etsy's US "sell" page is reported to describe Offsite Ads as a flat 15%, which conflicts with the 12% tier in the policy.
8. **Stripe's fee on your site** (2.9% + $0.30) is an assumption used for one comparison only.
9. **Quantity discounts.** I could not confirm that Etsy's Sales and Discounts tool can do "every 4th item free", which is why Listing 3 is a fixed set.
10. **Category and attribute names** are from memory of the listing form, not verified.
11. **Maximum processing time for made-to-order items**: no cap was found. Whatever you enter becomes the ship-by date Etsy holds you to.
12. **Package inserts** that mention your own site: not confirmed either way. The workflow above avoids them.
13. **How Etsy's "mark as gift" option behaves** (the packing-slip sentence in Listing 2).
