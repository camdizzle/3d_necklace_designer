## Read this first: what the rules allow

1. **External links and promotion are restricted.** MakerWorld's Community Guidelines list advertising and "links or scan code to non-MakerWorld related businesses, organizations, online stores, or websites" as prohibited, along with promotional links, and say not to put advertising or links in your account name, bio or pictures. Forum threads quote a carve-out for links "related to a specific 3D model", including sites that provide modelling services related to that model, and Bambu Lab staff have said 3D-printing-related links in model descriptions are "temporarily" allowed and judged case by case. I could not confirm that carve-out on the official page. So the drafts do this:
   - **No URL, QR code or site name in titles, tags, images or your profile bio.**
   - **Default description (Version A): one plain sentence saying how the model was made, with no link.** It is there as disclosure, not as an advert.
   - **Optional Version B adds one link line.** Use it only after you have read the current guidelines and are satisfied a link to the tool that made this specific model is allowed. If a moderator removes it, go back to Version A and do not re-add it.
   - Nothing in the drafts mentions prices, the $25 printed chain, or "order one". That would be advertising a store and is the clearest thing the rule forbids.
2. **Each page has to stand on its own as a useful free model.** The drafts lead with what the file is and how to print it. The mention of the designer is the last line.
3. **Tool-generated models: no rule found against them.** The rules I found target AI-generated models (which must be flagged at upload and are excluded from the Exclusive programme) and repeat uploads of identical files. These files come from your own parametric designer, not from generative AI, so the AI flag should not apply. I could not confirm how moderators treat generator output. To stay clear of the duplicate-upload rule, the four pages are written to be clearly different models (different text, colours, and two different chains) and the posting order spaces them out.
4. **Brand names.** I found no rule banning your own brand name. The rules that exist prohibit misleading brand terms, anything implying official endorsement, and MakerWorld or Bambu Lab logos; forum reports show models removed over third-party trademarks in titles and descriptions. The drafts use only generic terms ("Cuban link", "name chain", "GG") and keep "Chain Studio" out of titles and tags. Do not add printer brand names to titles to chase search.
5. **Real print photos.** Featured-model guidance requires a photo of the actual printed model, and older guideline versions require your own photo of the real print for CC0 uploads. Use a real photo as the cover; renders go after it and are labelled as renders.
6. **Not metal.** These print in plastic. The drafts say "gold tone" and "silver tone" filament throughout.

## Licence suggestion

**Suggested: Standard Digital File License** for all four.

Reported terms: personal use only; downloaders may print it for themselves, but may not sell prints, share the file elsewhere, or post remixes. Reasons:

- These are samples of a paid product. Your site's terms let buyers of a $2 file sell their prints; keeping the free samples personal-use keeps that distinction.
- It stops the files being re-uploaded to other model sites.
- You can loosen a licence later. Tightening one after people have downloaded under looser terms does not bind them.

Alternative if reach matters more than control: **CC BY-NC** (credit required, no commercial use, remixes allowed). Remixes link back to your model, which can help discovery. Avoid CC0 and CC BY unless you are happy for others to sell prints of these designs.

Three checks before you choose:

1. **Fonts.** The lettering is geometry made from font files (the samples use Archivo Black, Sour Gummy, Carter One and a bubble font). `[OWNER: confirm each font's licence allows distributing 3D models made from it]`
2. **Chain meshes.** `[OWNER: confirm the Cuban link and Twisted Star link meshes are your own design, or that their licence allows redistribution under the licence you pick]`
3. **Exclusive Model Programme.** It reportedly requires the Standard licence and exclusivity to MakerWorld. These designs can also be exported from your own site, so do not enrol them unless you have read the programme agreement and it permits that. I did not verify the terms.

The exact licence names in the upload form may differ. Pick the closest match.

## Posting order

Post one at a time, with a few days between each. I found no rule setting a minimum gap; the reason is that four similar chains uploaded in one sitting look like bulk uploads of one model, and repeat uploads of identical files are prohibited. Spacing them also lets you fix the page template after the first one.

| Order | Model | Why this position |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | your-hype-chain | The house style and the default design. Most colour parts, so it shows the multi-colour 3MF best. Use it to get the description, photos and print profile right before repeating them. |
| 2 | king-twisted-star | The only file with the Twisted Star chain, so it is the most different from model 1 and the least likely to read as a duplicate. Also needs the fewest colours, so more people can print it. |
| 3 | gg-gamer-tag | A different audience (gaming) and the most colour contrast. Post once the first two pages have real makes or comments you can learn from. |
| 4 | best-dad-silver | Seasonal. It will work as a general gift model now, but it earns most in the weeks before Father's Day. Post it then, or post now and update the photos and summary in May. |

Before posting model 1:

- [ ] Test-print all four files and fill in every `[OWNER]` blank
- [ ] Take real photos of each print (cover image must be a real print)
- [ ] Check every image for URLs, QR codes and watermarks
- [ ] Confirm font and chain mesh licences
- [ ] Read the current Community Guidelines and decide between footer Version A and Version B
- [ ] Make sure your MakerWorld profile name, bio and avatar carry no link or advertising
- [ ] At upload, choose "Original" (not "Remix"), and leave the AI-generated flag off unless generative AI was actually used

After posting: answer print questions, update the settings block when people report what worked on other printers, and do not post "check out my new model" link-only posts in the community feed, which the guidelines list as prohibited self-promotion.
